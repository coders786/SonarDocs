# English Examination Standards & Typography Guide
Version: 2.1.0-en
Language: English
Category: Examination Paper Structure & Formatting

## 1. Document Layout
- High-contrast Latin typography (Arial, Calibri, Times New Roman).
- Hierarchical sectioning: Section A (Multiple Choice Questions), Section B (Short Response Questions), Section C (Essay/Comprehensive Problems).
- Question marks denoted at right margins: `[5 Marks]`, `(10 marks)`.
- Standard Western numerals `1, 2, 3...` and alphanumeric subsections `(a), (b), (c)`.

## 2. Formatting Normalization
- Normalizing table columns, matching items, and fill-in-the-blank underline markers.
- Multi-part mathematical and scientific formula conservation.
