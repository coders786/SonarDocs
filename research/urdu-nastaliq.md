# Urdu Nastaliq Handwriting & Transcription Guide
Version: 2.1.0-ur
Language: Urdu (اردو)
Category: Nastaliq Ligatures & Secondary Board Standards

## 1. Nastaliq Baseline & Cascading Ligatures
- Urdu examination papers use Nastaliq, characterized by a sloping baseline cascading from top-right to bottom-left.
- In rapid handwriting, complex multi-tier ligatures compress vertically.
- "س" (Seen) and "ش" (Sheen) frequently drawn as a single smooth stroke without individual teeth.
- Distinguish between "ی" (Choti Yeh) and "ے" (Badi Yeh) in final word positions.
- Distinguish between "ن" (Nun with dot) and "ں" (Nun-Ghunna nasalized ending).

## 2. Standard Examination Hierarchies
- Header: Board of Intermediate and Secondary Education (BISE) / Secondary School Certificate (SSC).
- Sections:
  - حصہ اول: معروضی سوالات (MCQs)
  - حصہ دوم: مختصر جوابات کے سوالات (Short Questions)
  - حصہ سوم: تفصیلی جوابات کے سوالات (Long Descriptive Questions)
- Marks: Displayed right-aligned or margin-aligned in Eastern Arabic numerals: "(۵ نمبر)", "(۱۰ نمبر)".
