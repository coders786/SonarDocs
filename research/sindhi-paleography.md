## 1. Sindhi Extended 52-Character Orthography
Sindhi adds 24 unique letters to the standard 28 Arabic letters. Accurate transcription requires precise discrimination of dot positions and count:

### Four-Dot Formations (Quadrilateral vs In-line)
- ٽ (ṭe): Retroflex T with 4 dots in diamond or square above. Must not be confused with ت (2 dots) or ٿ (4 dots arranged differently).
- ٺ (ṭhe): Aspirated retroflex T with 4 dots arranged vertically or in horizontal pairs.
- ٿ (the): Aspirated dental T with 4 dots in a quad above.
- ڄ (ǰim): Affricate with 4 dots inside the loop. Must never be confused with ج (1 dot) or چ (3 dots).
- ڃ (ñim): Palatal nasal with 2 horizontal or 4 tiny dots inside the curve.

### Implosive Consonants (Sindhi Distinctive Phonemes)
- ٻ (ɓe): Bilabial implosive with two horizontal dots underneath the base.
- ڄ (ʄe): Palatal implosive with dots inside.
- ڳ (ɠe): Velar implosive with two distinct upper parallel bars.
- ڱ (ŋe): Velar nasal with gaaf stroke and dots.
- ڏ (ɗe): Alveolar implosive dal with special dotting.
- ڻ (ṇe): Retroflex nasal nun with characteristic upper accent/dots.

## 2. Handwritten Paleographic Variations
- Continuous strokes: In fast examination handwriting, teachers often draw horizontal ligatures without discrete shoshas (teeth).
- In Sindhi Naskh script, baseline remains horizontal, unlike cascading Urdu Nastaliq.
- Question markers: "سوال نمبر ۱:", "سوال نمبر ۲:", "جواب لکو:", "(نمبر: ۵)".
- Section headings: "حصو الف: ڪثير الانتخابي سوال", "حصو ب: مختصر سوال", "حصو ج: تفصيلي سوال".
